How the club is run

The club is run by its members under its own written rules.

  • Officers look after the day-to-day work: recording contributions, keeping the books and answering members’ questions.
  • Two people for important steps. Changes that matter are made by one officer and approved by another.
  • The board oversees the officers and approves the decisions the rules reserve for it.
  • Open records. Every change is logged, and each member can see their own account at any time.
