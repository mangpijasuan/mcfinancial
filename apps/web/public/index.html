What the club is about

A club of people who know each other, decide together, and keep every member’s account open to that member.

Built on trust

Members join through people they know, and the club’s decisions are made by its members under its own rules.

Clear records

Every contribution is recorded with a numbered receipt, and each member can see their own account and statements online.

Run by members

Officers do the day-to-day work, important decisions need a second person to approve them, and the board oversees the whole.
