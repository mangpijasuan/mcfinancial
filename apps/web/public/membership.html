Being a member

What members do, and what they can expect from the club.

  1. Contribute as agreed. Members contribute regularly, as set out in the club’s rules. Each contribution gets a numbered receipt.
  2. See your own account. Members sign in online to see their contributions and statements, and to make payments.
  3. Take part. Members take part in the club’s meetings and decisions, as the rules provide.
  4. Keep in touch. Members keep their contact details up to date so the club can reach them.
