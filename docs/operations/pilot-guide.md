# Pilot guide

A one-month trial with the officers and a small group of members, before everyone is invited. Start once the [go-live checklist](go-live-checklist.md) is done.

**During the pilot the club's current sheet and process stay the official record.** The app runs alongside them, and the month ends by comparing the two.

## The plan

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | The three officers (Finance, Treasurer, Board), and 6–10 members |
| **The members** | At least one with a loan, one co-signer, one who pays by Zelle, one by cash and one by auto-pay; iPhone and Android users; one or two who are not comfortable with apps |
| **How long** | One full calendar month, including its month-end (for example 1–31 December) |
| **What is used** | Members: signing in, Home and the membership card, Payment History with receipts and Year by year, My Loan, Statements, and Zelle payment claims. Officers: recording contributions and repayments, approvals, the monthly report |
| **What stays off** | Card and bank payments online, late fees, email, the ledger cutover ([checklist §8](go-live-checklist.md#8-left-off-on-purpose)) |
| **Support** | One officer is the contact for the pilot group (a phone number or a group chat) |

## Before day 1 (officers)

1. Choose the pilot members and ask them. Tell them it is a trial, that their figures are being checked, and how to report anything wrong.
2. For each pilot member, the Treasurer checks their figures once more (contributions, *Year by year*, loan).
3. Give each pilot member access: *Members* → the member → **Portal access** → set a temporary password → **Enable access**. Give them the password in person or by phone, never in a group message.
4. Give each one the member page below (print it, or send it on its own).

## During the month (officers)

**Every time money comes in**, record it in the app the same day, as well as on the sheet:
- *Contributions* → **Record payment** (cash, check, Zelle received directly);
- *Repayments* → **Record repayment**;
- *Online Payment Review* → confirm the Zelle payments members claimed in the app, once the money shows in the bank.

Many payments at once (for example a month of auto-pay): *Administration* → **Bulk Upload**, using the sample file.

**A payment recorded by mistake** is reversed, never deleted: *Contributions* → the payment → **Reverse**, and the Treasurer approves it in *Approvals*.

**Weekly (Treasurer, 10 minutes):**
- *Approvals*: nothing waiting.
- *Online Payment Review*: no claim older than a few days.
- Note any question or problem from the pilot group.

## Month-end (Treasurer)

1. Record the bank balance from the statement (*Money → Treasury*).
2. *Contributions* → **Monthly report** → the month → **Download PDF**.
3. Compare it with the club's sheet for the same month, member by member. **Every amount must match to the cent.** For each difference, find which side is wrong and correct it (in the app: reverse and re-record).
4. Sign the report's sign-off lines; a second officer reviews it.

## Feedback

Ask each pilot member after the first week and at the end:

1. Could you sign in without help? If not, what happened?
2. Were your figures right (payments, total, loan)? If not, which?
3. What was confusing, or missing?
4. Which phone and browser did you use?

Keep the answers in one list, with what was done about each.

## Did it work?

Invite everyone when all of these hold:

- [ ] The month's report matched the club's sheet to the cent (after corrections, each explained).
- [ ] No pilot member found a wrong figure that the Treasurer had not already corrected.
- [ ] Every pilot member signed in, on their own phone, without an officer's help (or with help once, at the start).
- [ ] The officers recorded the whole month's payments in the app without needing a developer.
- [ ] A backup restore check passed during the month.
- [ ] No serious problem in the feedback is still open.

If one does not hold, fix it and run one more month with the same group.

## After the pilot

1. Invite the rest of the members in batches of 15–20 a week, so the officers can answer questions.
2. Decide when the app becomes the official record and the sheet stops, at a month boundary.
3. Then, each when ready: bank payments through QuickBooks, email, the domain's public site, and the ledger cutover once the accountant has approved the chart of accounts.

## For pilot members: getting started

*Print or send this part on its own.*

**Millionaires Club member app — trial**

Thank you for trying the club's new member app. For this month it runs alongside the usual process; your payments are still recorded the usual way too.

**Sign in**
- On your phone, open: **app.mcfinancial.us** *(or the address your officer gives you)*
- Member ID: **MC-_____** · Password: the one your officer gave you
- Tip: add it to your home screen (iPhone: Share → *Add to Home Screen*; Android: menu → *Add to Home screen*).

**What to check**
- **Home**: this month's dues, whether you can borrow, and **Download membership card**.
- **History**: your contributions this year with their receipts, and *Year by year* for earlier years. Do they match what you paid?
- **Loan** (if you have one): what you have paid, what is left, and your next payment.
- **More**: your statements, and loan agreements to sign if you have a loan.

**Paying by Zelle**
- **Pay** → *Zelle*: copy the club's Zelle details into your bank's app and send the payment. Then come back, enter the Zelle confirmation number, and tap **Submit Zelle claim**. An officer confirms it once the money arrives.

**Something wrong or confusing?**
Tell _______________ (phone: _______________). Please say what you saw and, if you can, send a screenshot. Never send your password to anyone; officers will never ask for it.
